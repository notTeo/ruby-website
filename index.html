About

Ruby is a cocktail bar built around one idea: a well-made drink has nothing to hide behind. We keep the list short, stir and shake to order, and squeeze the citrus fresh every evening.

Come for a Dry Martini at the bar, or settle into a corner for the night. Either way, the door stays open until late.
